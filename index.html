Why NutriMatch Was Created

For years, I was trapped in a frustrating cycle with doctors who wouldn't listen. I was handed a "garbage can" diagnosis of IBS—which I believe is one of the most overdiagnosed conditions in modern medicine—along with chronic excess acid.

Before long, I was put on a running prescription for Omeprazole, a harsh medication with severe long-term side effects. Between the symptoms and the side effects, I couldn’t even keep a full-time job. I knew I had reached a breaking point: something had to change.

I started monitoring everything I ate, but it felt like everything made me react. Desperate for answers, I took a food allergy test and cut out every trigger food. Almost instantly, my energy levels soared. That breakthrough sparked an obsession with deep research into food science, cellular bioavailability, and targeted exercise.

By rebuilding my diet from the ground up, I balanced my hormones, healed my gut, and got my life back. NutriMatch was born to save you from the years of trial, error, and medical dismissal I went through—giving you direct, exact pathways to food as true healing.

Gut-Brain Axis Published October 2026

Short-Chain Fatty Acids (SCFAs) and Neuro-Inflammation

Recent findings highlight how fermentable fibers from legumes and cooked/cooled resistant starches yield high concentrations of butyrate in the colon. Butyrate directly influences gut lining integrity, reducing systemic low-grade inflammation often misdiagnosed as functional acid reflux or IBS.

Nutrient Synergies Published September 2026

Piperine and Curcumin Bioavailability Enhancement

Clinical trials confirm that co-administering piperine (active compound in black pepper) with curcumin (turmeric) inhibits hepatic glucuronidation, raising serum curcumin bioavailability by up to 2,000%.

Micronutrient Timing Published August 2026

Magnesium Glycinate vs. Citrate for Sleep & Acid Sensitivity

Comparative study indicates that highly chelated magnesium glycinate minimizes gastrointestinal motility irritation compared to oxide or citrate forms, making it optimal for individuals prone to gastric sensitivity.
